Our Story & Heart

In-Tah Cafe began with a simple love story between a married couple who shared a passion for coffee and café dates. Over cups of coffee, they shared conversations, laughter, and dreams. Wanting to share that same warmth and happiness with the community, their dream became a reality in Barangay Sagana, Isabela.

Our Mission

To serve quality coffee and delicious food while creating a welcoming space where people can connect, share stories, and make lasting memories.

Our Core Values

Quality: Beverages and food prepared with care. | Hospitality: Warmth and genuine care. | Connection: Strengthening relationships.
